# Agent IQ (wave 26, PHASE23 IQ): the water audio that did not play, and the silent exit death

The user heard no water when the boat is lowered into the water, along the ride and while the lock fills. Every water,
landing and lock event of the route **was posted** (no poster is missing); the in-memory sounds among them **had no media**:
33 `Bank_*` banks of the opening were prepared structure-only because `UAkBank::Load` / `Unload` tested the wrong flag
(`GenerateDefinition`, mask 2) where retail tests `AutoGenerated` (mask 4 of +60). Retail loads every non-auto-generated bank
with its media and prepares only the auto-generated `Bk_*` dialogue banks. Fixed; the same defect put the exit purge on the `UnPrepareBank` path
for all 33 banks (where 3 of 16 IH4 exits died) and made the continuous ambience containers churn through media-less items
(1,133 "next item" posts in IH4's run, 181 now). Retail's `UEngine::PreExit` (2013 0x1d8850) also stops every sound before the exit purge; ported.
19 of 19 exits with this package's two builds are clean.

Worktree `build/agentIQ_wt` (made at `224ee15`), build dir `build/agentIQ` (RelWithDebInfo), staged as
`DishonoredGame_IQ.exe`. Two DishonoredGame builds: build 1 = diagnostics + the bank flag (the first ninja invocation stopped
on a compile error in the new dump writer after 1,286 of 1,299 steps; the resumed invocation compiled that file and
`akbank.cpp` and linked, 15 steps, so it is counted as build 1), build 2 = `UEngine::PreExit` + the diag gain metric. One
CoreSmoke build. Private IDA copies `build/agentIQ/idb/` (2013 VA = rva + 0x400000). Tools `build/agentIQ/tools/`, runs and
dumps `build/agentIQ/exits/`, WAVs / A/B / spectra `build/agentIQ/ab/`, decoded media `build/agentIQ/media/`. No fmodel, no
real input, no retail exe. Nothing committed. Sync script `build/agentIQ_sync.py` (base `224ee15`).

## 5. The exit death: what it was

IH4's three dying runs (IH4t/v/w) end right after the 27th unprepare (`WwiseDefaultBank_WwiseGlobal.bnk`) and two
`UHttpRequestWindows::BeginDestroy` lines, in the second where clean runs go on to unprepare the `Bank_Amb_*` banks; the
process really ended (IH4v: log ends 166.08, cutscene_compare wall 170.3 s; the harness never kills). Every bank in that
window was one of the 33 structure-only `Bank_*` banks: the exit purge was *unpreparing* HIRC structure (`UnPrepareBank`)
under voices of the same banks. Whole runs churned: the continuous ambience containers posted their next item up to 38 times
a second (IH4p 1,133 "next posted" lines, IH4t/v/w 832 / 764 / 1,233) because each item was an in-memory sound without media
that failed at once. The churn is not the trigger by itself - IH4v has 0 such posts after `Preparing to exit`, IH4w 24 - but
both disappear with the bank fix: no `Bank_*` bank is unprepared at exit any more (they are `UnloadBank`ed, the path that
stops the voices of the slot first), the containers advance once per item (181 lines), and with retail's `UEngine::PreExit`
every voice is stopped before `CleanupWorld` and the purge (0 posts and 0 next-item posts after `Preparing to exit` in all 19
runs). No dump was ever written, so the exact access of the old death is not named; the `-akdump` writer is armed for the next.

## 6. Where the brief was wrong (with the measurement)

1. **"Find why the water events do not play ... which never post"**: all of them post, in IH4 already (IH4p: Chain_Lift,
   Lift_Water_Impact, Amb_Water_Lock, Amb_Water_Lock_Wheel x6, Waterfall x2, Water_Lap_Dock x4, Lift_End at the matinee keys).
   The cause is not Wwise playback, a missing poster, 3D attenuation or the propagation path but the game-side bank load
   (`UAkBank::Load` read mask 2 instead of 4): 33 `Bank_*` banks without media.
2. **"continuous ambience containers still post their next item during the bank unload"** is a symptom of the same defect: the
   containers churned because their items had no media (1,133 vs 181 next-item posts per run), and it is not what the dying runs
   share - IH4v has none after `Preparing to exit`; what they share is the `UnPrepareBank` of structure-only `Bank_*` banks.
3. **The landing "~t 100-110"**: there is no landing event there. Retail t 100-110 is world 142.6-152.6 = the end of the lock
   lift (Snd_Motorboat_Small_Lift_End at w142.4); the boat lands in the water (Lift_Water_Impact) at world 63.0 = retail t
   20.4; the boat stops at the tower at world 94.1. retail.mp4 covers all of them and the lock fill (w120-144).
4. **IH4's sub-150 Hz figure** ("retail 48.8 against our 36.3 in w86-125, fork G's spectra") came from
   `build/agentIH4/G/tools/spectra.py`, which unpacks `anchors()` as (sink, logt, world) - swapped - and reads IH3b's capture
   (`IH3b.wav` / `CC_IH3b.log` hard-coded). Corrected (`build/agentIQ/tools/spectra.py`): IH4p 38.3 against 48.8, B00 37.6.
   The deficit is real; the numbers were not IH4's.
5. **"w60 ... our ambience and music start at 60.4 / 62.1"**: those are log times; world = log - 9.7 on these runs, so the
   ambience and music start at world ~50.7 / ~52.4. Retail's w60 peak is a single 250 ms transient at w60.75 (-14.6 dBFS
   against -26..-30 around it); ours has no post within 0.7 s of it (a dialogue line at w60.06). Not matched to an event.
6. **The 194 unported functions**: none is on the path of a water voice.
7. **"10 of 10 exits after a minidump names the access"**: no exit died in 19 runs of the two builds or in 8 debugger-attached
   runs of the IH4 exe, so there was no dump to name; the fix is the measured churn plus retail's exit order.
